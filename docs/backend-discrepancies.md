Severity: 🔴 breaks the client or a game rule · 🟡 contract or naming drift · ⚪ cosmetic or missing docs.

## Offline campaign: what the backend must provide (decision 0046 #2)

The client plays campaign levels on the device with its own engine and the bundled `packages/content`, keeps finished attempts in an outbox, and sends them when online (Part 1 §9.3, Part 8 §1.3, §6.4, decisions 0016, 0018). The server checks each attempt by replaying its taps against the same level. Required:

1. **Content parity.** The server holds the same levels as `packages/content`: level v2 JSON (`contracts/schemas/level.schema.json`), ids `c-1…c-N`, the same layouts, limits, and stars; a global optional `contentVersion` (decision 0016 #2). Rows K28–K30.
2. **Replay engine parity.** The Go replay gives the same result as `packages/engine` for every level: all three probe modes, bombs (2 moves), buoys (+3), fog, target order, mine mark, win before the limit check, one continue (+3, stars capped at 1). Proven by running the shared vectors `packages/engine/vectors/*.json` in Go tests (Part 6 §9). Rows K15, K19, K26.
3. **`POST /attempts:batch`** per Part 7 §4 (row K2): up to 50 attempts, idempotent by `attemptId`, statuses `accepted | duplicate | corrected | rejected | late`; an attempt older than 7 days is `late` — progress counts, no currency (K5); rewards per Part 1 §3.6 and Part 6 §5.5 (K20); the response carries `progress` and `wallet`.
4. **Offline continue** inside an attempt: `continued: true, continueMethod: "emeralds"` → replay applies it at the loss and charges ◆ 15 unless `POST /continue` was already paid for that `attemptId` (decision 0018 #1; K7).
5. **`GET /progress`** → `{currentLevel, levels: [{id, bestStars, bestMoves}], worldsCompleted}`; the client merges it by maximum (decision 0016 #1; K9).
6. **No server-issued packs or encryption** for bundled levels (K10) and no rejection of first-tap wins by a minimum game time (K6) — both wait for the developer's decision.
7. **Daily mode** (Part 6 §6.3, Part 7 §4 `/daily/{date}`, `/daily/history`, `/streak`, `/streak/restore`): the same seeded generator as the engine, attempts with `mode: "daily"`, streak and its restore. Not present in the backend at all.
